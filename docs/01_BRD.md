## 1. Business Context
**MavenFlix** is a video-streaming service operating with a subscription-based business model, where customers pay a recurring fees to gain access to its services.
As a subscription-based business, MavenFlix's performance depends partly on its ability to acquire new customers and retain existing subscribers. Customer retention plays an important role in maintaining a sustainable subscriber base and supporting recurring revenue potential.
Understanding subscription growth, cancellation trends, and customer retention patterns can help management evaluate business performance, identify potential retention challenges, and make informed decisions about customer engagement and retention initiatives.

**Assumptions:**
- Customers are charged at the beginning of their respective billing period.
- Subscription automatically renew at the start of each billing period unless canceled by the users.
- Customers who canceled their subscription retain access until the end of their paid billing period.
- Subscription prices remain unchanged thrroughout the observed subscription period unless available data indicates otherwise.
## 2. Business Problem
MavenFlix management currently lacks a consolidated view of subscription performance, making it difficult to assess the current state of the business and evaluate performance over time.
Although subscription data are available, they are not yet consolidated into consistent and actionable insights. In particular, management has limited visibility into subscription growth, cancellation trends, and customer retention patterns across different periods and subscription cohorts.
Without clearly defined performance metrics, management may struggle to identify potential retention challenges, evaluate changes in the subscriber base, and prioritize areas for further investigation, limiting the company's ability to make informed, data-driven decisions.
## 3. Project Objectives
### Objective 1 - Evaluate Subscription Growth and Performance
Analyze changes in MavenFlix's subscription base over the available reporting period by measuring new subscriptions, active subscription, and cancellations to identify overall growth trends and fluctuations.
### Objective 2 - Analyze Subscription Cancellation Pattern
Measure subscription cancellation rates. Examine how cancellation patterns vary across reporting periods and available subscription characteristics to identify potential retention challenges and areas requiring further investigation.
### Objective 3 - Evaluate Subscription Retention Through Cohort Analysis
Analyze and compare subscription retention rates across different signup cohorts to identify retention patterns over time and provide data-driven insights that can inform future customer retention initiatives.
